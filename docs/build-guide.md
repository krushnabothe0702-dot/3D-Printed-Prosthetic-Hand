# Build guide

Recommended sequence, as followed for this prototype.

1. **Print one finger first** and check that the hinge moves freely.
2. **Test the cord path and elastic return on the bench**, without attaching the hand to a person.
3. **Print the remaining parts** — finger segments, thumb, palm, gauntlet and pins. Use supports for overhangs and remove them carefully.
4. **Assemble the joints** with the pins; each joint should swing freely without wobble.
5. **Route the tendons** from each fingertip, along the palm side, through the palm to the gauntlet.
6. **Fit the return elastics** on the back of each finger.
7. **Tension and tie off** each tendon at the gauntlet so all fingers start to close at the same wrist angle.
8. **Bench test**: bend the wrist slowly and check that all five fingers close and reopen.
9. **Tabletop grasp test** with light objects (cup, ball, bottle). Record what works and what slips.

## Tuning tips

- A finger that closes late needs a tighter tendon; one that never fully opens needs a looser tendon or stronger elastic.
- Retie knots after the first few cycles — new cord stretches.
- Sand rough cord channels so the cord does not fray.

## Safety

Educational, non-clinical prototype. Avoid tight straps and heavy loads. Do not wear it on a limb without review by a qualified prosthetist or engineer.
